# Discount Guidelines (Field Edition)

## Decision tree

1. **Is the customer on a Two-year contract?**
   YES → do NOT discount. Offer a free add-on (TechSupport, OnlineSecurity, Streaming bundle) instead.
2. **Is the customer on Month-to-month with tenure < 12?**
   YES → 15% discount for 12 months CONTINGENT on switch to One-year contract. This is the highest-ROI standard play.
3. **Is the customer's monthly charge > $90 and they have 3+ tickets?**
   YES → 10% discount + Priority Support Queue + named CSM. Discount alone underperforms here; pair it with a service fix.
4. **Is the customer a SeniorCitizen with low feature usage?**
   YES → free service bundle, not discount. Senior NPS lifts more on "safety" than on "savings".
5. **Otherwise** → start with non-discount offer (free add-on, support upgrade).

## Discount size by competitive context

| Context                              | Recommended Discount | Notes                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| No competitor mentioned              | 5–10%                | Avoid signaling weakness.                  |
| Competitor mentioned, no quote       | 10–15%               | Match perceived alternative price.         |
| Written quote from named competitor  | 15–25%               | Match or undercut by ~5%.                  |
| Customer has cancelled, in win-back  | 20–30%               | Acceptance drops to ~10%; high cost.       |

## Discount fatigue rules

- A customer may receive a retention discount at most once every 6 months.
- A single account may not have >50% of its monthly charge discounted across stacked offers.
- All retention discounts auto-expire; no permanent rate changes via this program.
