# Customer Retention Policies (v3.2 - effective 2026-Q1)

## 1. Discount Authority Matrix

| Tier   | Max Discount % | Max Duration | Approval Required |
| ------ | -------------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| CSM    | 10%            | 3 months     | None              |
| Lead   | 20%            | 6 months     | CSM Manager       |
| Manager| 30%            | 12 months    | RVP               |
| RVP    | 40%            | 24 months    | VP Customer Success |

Discounts above 30% require a documented competitive-threat reason (named competitor, signed quote, or written cancellation request).

## 2. Eligibility Gates

- Account must be in good standing (no >60d overdue invoices).
- Customer must NOT have received a retention discount in the last 6 months.
- Customers under a price-locked contract (Two year) are NOT eligible for discounts; offer service upgrades instead (free TechSupport, free StreamingTV/Movies).

## 3. Non-Discount Offers

| Offer                       | Cost to Company | When to Use                                  |
| --------------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Free TechSupport (6 months) | $30/customer    | Customers with 2+ tickets in last 90 days    |
| Free OnlineSecurity         | $20/customer    | Security-conscious or senior citizens        |
| Free OnlineBackup           | $20/customer    | Customers who churned a competitor for data loss |
| Free DeviceProtection       | $25/customer    | High monthly charges + new device complaints |
| Free StreamingTV+Movies     | $60/customer    | Entertainment-driven cohort, low engagement  |
| Dedicated CSM               | $400/customer/yr| High-Value + High-Risk segment only          |
| Priority Support Queue      | $50/customer/yr | Customers with avg response time > 12h       |

## 4. Communication Rules

- **First touch must be a call**, not email, for any High-Risk customer with NPS ≤ 6.
- Email follow-up must be sent within 24 hours of the call summarizing what was agreed.
- Never reference the churn score or NPS directly with the customer.

## 5. Escalation

If retention call fails (customer still intends to cancel), escalate to a
Retention Specialist with full SHAP report attached. Specialist may offer
the next-tier discount one time.
