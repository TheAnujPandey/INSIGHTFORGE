# Past Successful Retention Campaigns

## Campaign A - "Fiber Loyalty Lock-in" (2025 Q2)
- **Target cohort:** Fiber optic + Month-to-month + tenure < 12 + Monthly > $80
- **Offer:** 15% discount for 12 months in exchange for switch to One year contract.
- **Channel:** Outbound call by senior CSM, followed by DocuSign within 48h.
- **Results:** 1,820 accounts touched, 64% accepted, 12-month retention 81% (vs 38% control). ARR retained: $1.6M.
- **What worked:** Reframing the offer as "you keep your speed, we lock the price" rather than "sign a longer contract."

## Campaign B - "Quiet Senior Win-Back" (2025 Q3)
- **Target cohort:** SeniorCitizen=1 + OnlineSecurity=No + 2+ tickets in 90 days
- **Offer:** Free OnlineSecurity + TechSupport bundle for 12 months, no contract change required.
- **Channel:** Call + mailed confirmation letter (this cohort engages with paper mail).
- **Results:** 540 accounts touched, 71% accepted, 12-month retention 86%. ARR retained: $410k.
- **Insight:** Senior citizens respond more to *safety* framing than *discount* framing.

## Campaign C - "Frustrated Fiber Save" (2025 Q4)
- **Target cohort:** Fiber + ticket_count >= 4 + avg_response_time > 12h + sentiment = Negative
- **Offer:** Priority Support Queue + assigned CSM + 10% discount for 6 months.
- **Channel:** Call within 4 business hours of trigger.
- **Results:** 290 accounts touched, 58% accepted. Acceptance was lower because customers had already started shopping competitors. Of acceptors, 12-month retention 79%.
- **Lesson:** Speed matters. Adding "within 4 hours" SLA increased acceptance by 22 pts vs. the next-day variant.

## Campaign D - "Two-Year Auto-Pay Sweetener" (2025 Q4)
- **Target cohort:** Tenure > 24 + Electronic check payment + still month-to-month
- **Offer:** $5/mo discount for switching to autopay + One-year contract.
- **Channel:** Email + in-app banner.
- **Results:** 4,100 accounts touched, 27% accepted (low-effort, low-cost campaign). Reduced involuntary churn by 31% in cohort.

## Campaigns that DID NOT work

- "Universal 5% off" emails: <2% acceptance, signaled weakness to no-intent-to-churn customers.
- Pure win-back after cancellation: 8% acceptance vs 60%+ when intercepted pre-cancellation.
- Generic LLM-written emails without segment-specific framing: -14% open rate vs templated.
